I'm a data analyst with a background in city and regional planning. At the Southern Coalition for Social Justice, I collect, analyze, map, and visualize data with grassroots groups and lawyers on voting rights, environmental justice, housing, and the justice system across the South.

I care most about economic justice, affordable housing, and community development, and about making the data useful to the people it describes.

      About me

      I think of data as one of the best storytelling tools we have. A number on its own rarely changes anyone's mind, but a map of who lost their polling place, or a chart of which neighborhoods flood first, can. I try to make every analysis something a community member could pick up and use to tell their own story.

      I'm originally from Raleigh, and the South means a lot to me. The communities I work with are ones I know and care about, and that's a big part of what motivates my work.

      I live in Durham. Soccer is a big part of my life. I play, I coach youth soccer in Durham, and I'm always watching the NC Courage and the USWNT.
